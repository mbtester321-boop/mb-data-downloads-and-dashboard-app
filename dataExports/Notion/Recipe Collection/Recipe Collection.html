🥥 Green Curry with Tofu
Ingredients
  • 1 block firm tofu, cubed
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • Bamboo shoots, Thai basil, bell pepper
Steps
1. Fry curry paste in a little coconut cream.
2. Add remaining coconut milk and vegetables; simmer.
3. Add tofu and basil; serve with jasmine rice.
🍛 Chana Masala
Ingredients
  • 2 cans chickpeas
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric
Steps
1. Sauté aromatics, bloom spices.
2. Add tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 20 min.
3. Finish with lemon and cilantro.
🍜 Miso Ramen
Ingredients
  • 1 L chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 2 portions fresh ramen noodles
  • Soft-boiled eggs, corn, scallions, nori
Steps
1. Warm stock and whisk in miso (don't boil).
2. Cook noodles separately.
3. Assemble bowls and add toppings.
🥞 Buttermilk Pancakes
Ingredients
  • 2 cups flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 cups buttermilk, 2 eggs, 3 tbsp melted butter
Steps
1. Whisk dry and wet separately, then combine (lumps are fine).
2. Cook on a buttered griddle until bubbles form, flip once.
🍝 Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
Ingredients
  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Parsley and parmesan to finish
Steps
1. Boil pasta in well-salted water.
2. Gently fry garlic in oil until just golden; add chili flakes.
3. Toss pasta with oil and a splash of pasta water; finish with parsley.
🌮 Chicken Tinga Tacos
Ingredients
  • 2 chicken breasts, poached and shredded
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 chipotles in adobo
  • Corn tortillas, cotija, crema
Steps
1. Blend tomatoes and chipotles.
2. Sauté onion, add sauce and simmer 10 min.
3. Fold in chicken and serve on warm tortillas.
🍰 Tiramisu
Ingredients
  • 500 g mascarpone
  • 4 eggs, separated, 100 g sugar
  • 300 ml strong espresso
  • Ladyfingers, cocoa powder
Steps
1. Beat yolks with sugar, fold in mascarpone, then whipped whites.
2. Dip ladyfingers in espresso and layer with cream.
3. Chill at least 6 hours; dust with cocoa.
